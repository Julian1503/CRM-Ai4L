# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Business sales representatives, client relationship managers, and system administrators who need to manage client data, track purchased services, import client spreadsheets, and sync newsletter subscriptions. They value speed, high legibility, and simple, error-free operations under varying light conditions.

## Product Purpose
A highly customized, visual CRM designed to manage client/prospect contacts, track catalog services, parse and import spreadsheet lists with dynamic column mapping, and synchronize newsletter subscription statuses bi-directionally with EmailOctopus.

## Brand Personality
*   **Voice**: Professional, reliable, clean, and highly precise.
*   **Adjectives**: Intuitive, premium, solid.
*   **Emotional Goals**: Evoke confidence in data synchronization and ease of use.
*   **Typography Pairing**: `Outfit` (headlines/display) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (body) for a sleek, editorial feel.
*   **Primary Theme**: Deep indigo with glassmorphic accents, focusing on contrast and clarity.

## Anti-references
*   Generic SaaS dashboard templates with overly cluttered grid layout cards.
*   Low contrast grey-on-grey text elements.
*   Side-stripe borders (e.g. thick left border) on cards.
*   Tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows above every section.

## Design Principles
1.  **Frictionless Clarity**: Information hierarchy is paramount. The difference between a Customer and a Prospect, or a Subscribed vs. Unsubscribed contact, should be visual and instant.
2.  **Data Safety & Visual Audit**: Importing files and syncing with APIs must show detailed progress and human-readable validation summaries before committing updates.
3.  **Refined Density**: Make dashboard cards and detail drawers spacious to allow easy scanning. Reject crowded layouts that increase cognitive load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
*   Targeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
*   Strict minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for body copy and placeholders.
*   Support keyboard navigation for drawers and import mapping dropdowns.
*   Respect browser/system `prefers-reduced-motion` settings with instant transitions.
